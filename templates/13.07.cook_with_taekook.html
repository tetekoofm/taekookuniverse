How the Game Works

  1. Customer arrives and chooses a dish from the menu.
  2. Check the recipe or skip directly to preparation.
  3. Collect ingredients in the mini-game.
  4. Cook the order with Tae & Koo’s help.
  5. Deliver the order to the customer.
  6. Everyone is happy!
  7. Continue with the next customer or exit the game.

How the Game Works

  1. Move Tete left or right with your mouse or finger.
  2. Catch only the required ingredients for the current recipe.
  3. Avoid incorrect ingredients.
  4. Take the ingredients back to Koo.
