CHAPTER 01

Why Donate?

As fans, there are many ways we can support Taehyung and Jung Kook, and one of the easiest is through donations. These donations are used solely for fan-led initiatives. Every contribution goes directly toward supporting fan-led projects. We maintain transparency by keeping records and receipts for transactions, which are available upon request.

Giveaways

We organize events and giveaways for fans to celebrate new releases and important milestones.

Streaming Parties

Funds are used to sponsor premium accounts on streaming platforms, which, in turn, help boost the streams.

Album Purchases

Donations are used to buy their singles or albums on platforms like iTunes, Amazon Music, Qobuz, and others to increase chart positions.

Voting Campaigns

We organize drives to sponsor memberships on voting apps, helping the artist and their songs reach #1 on various platforms.

Charity Projects

Occasionally, donations are collected for charity projects benefiting communities, all in the name of Taehyung and Jungkook.

CHAPTER 03

Frequently Asked Questions

How will I know my donation is being used appropriately?

Transparency is key, so we provide detailed records of all funds raised, along with receipts for purchases and campaigns. Every contribution under our control is carefully directed toward projects that support both artists, with no favoritism.

Can I donate for a specific project?

Yes. When a specific project is being funded, we provide a clear donation goal and your contribution will go toward that initiative.

Is there a minimum donation amount?

No. Any amount is greatly appreciated. Every bit counts, and small donations add up to make a big difference!

Will my donation be tax-deductible?

As this is a fan-led initiative, donations are not tax-deductible. However, we maintain transparency and provide records for all contributions.

  • Check official fanbase donation announcements.
  • Save donation receipts for transparency.
  • Support projects that match your interests.
  • Share donation campaigns with other fans.
  • Donate only through verified links.
