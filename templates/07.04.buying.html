CHAPTER 01

What Does Buying Music Mean?

Buying music refers to legally purchasing physical/digital copies of a single or an album from official platforms. These purchases contribute to sales charts, strengthen release performance, and help achieve important milestones.

CHAPTER 02

The Buyer's Checklist

Follow these steps to ensure your purchase is counted:

Choose the Right Platform

Purchase from official platforms like Weverse Shop, BTS Store, iTunes, Qobuz, Amazon, and Ktown4u.

Buy Individual Tracks & Albums

On digital platforms, buy individual songs first to maximize impact and then proceed to buy the album.

Avoid Bulk Purchases in One Go

Spread out your purchases using different accounts and payment methods instead of bulk-buying at once.

Use Different Platforms

Purchase from multiple platforms instead of focusing on just one.

Ensure Proper Payment

Use legitimate payment methods to avoid refunds or cancellations that could affect charting.

Download Immediately

After digital purchases, download the songs/albums immediately, to ensure the sale is counted. Also, delete them from your Library so your streams are counted.

Share & Recommend

Share the official buying links with friends and family to encourage them to purchase.

Join Buying Parties

If you are unsure, join the Buying Parties organized by our fanbases during the new release.
