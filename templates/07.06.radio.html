CHAPTER 02

How to Request Songs

Requesting their songs is simple. Follow station guidelines and make your requests count.

Request Forms

Most stations accept song requests through forms. Visit their website, navigate to the "Song Request" section, and fill out the form with the artist's name and the song title.

Social Media Requests

Many stations are active on platforms like X, Instagram, Facebook, and Bluesky. Use hashtags and comment on their posts to request Taehyung and Jung Kook’s songs.

Radio Apps

Stations with apps like iHeartRadio, TuneIn, or GetMeRadio.com often have "Song Request" features. Install the app, search for the station, and submit your request.

Live Requests

DJs love interacting with listeners during live shows. Call in and politely request your favorite song. Keep an eye on their announcements for when this is avilable.

Fanbase Campaigns

Our TK Radio fanbase organizes radio request campaigns. Join their projects!

Local Stations

Explore local radio stations and check their websites or social media for request options.

CHAPTER 03

Effective Requesting Tips

Follow these practices to support radio play effectively.

Be Polite

Always communicate professionally with radio stations and DJs.

Be Specific

Mention the exact song title and artist name in every request.

Avoid Spam

Do not send repeated requests within a short period.

Engage Naturally

Like, share, and interact with station content.

Spread Awareness

Encourage other fans worldwide to participate.

Join Projects

Participate in fanbase-led radio campaigns and challenges.
