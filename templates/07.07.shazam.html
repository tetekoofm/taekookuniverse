CHAPTER 04

Different Ways to Shazam

There are multiple ways to Shazam Taehyung and Jung Kook's songs. Choose the method that works best for you.

Manual Shazam

Open the Shazam app, play their songs from your preferred streaming platform, and tap the Shazam button.

Available on:
Android
iOS
macOS

Auto Shazam

Long press the Shazam button to activate Auto Shazam. Let the app detect multiple songs automatically.

After completion, open each Shazam result once before removing them from your library.

Browser Extension

Install the Shazam Chrome extension on Chrome or Edge. Pin the extension for easy access.

Play Taehyung and Jung Kook's songs in your browser and click the extension button.

Auto Shazam is not available with this method.

Shazam Website

Open the Shazam webpage and play songs from another browser tab.

Click the Shazam button and keep the page active until the song is detected.

Auto Shazam is not available with this method.
