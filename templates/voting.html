CHAPTER 04

Types of Voting

Voting can be done either through an app or website poll. There are also different kinds of votings

Pre-Voting

Conducted before an award show with a list of nominations from which top ranks are chosen for Live voting.

Live Voting

Real-time voting during broadcasts where every second counts.

Timed Voting

Some platforms allow voting within a timeframe, as in, once per day per account or once per hour etc.,

Unlimited Voting

Certain platforms allow continuous voting without daily limits.

CHAPTER 05

The Voting Checklist

Follow the correct steps for doing it the right way

Find the Platform

Find the voting platform where Tae or Koo is nominated. Make sure they are an official platform.

Create an Account

Some platforms require an account to be able to vote. So, register using a valid email and complete verification.

Read the Rules

Go through the platform specific guidelines and follow them to the T.

Vote Carefully

Find the appropriate category where they are nominated and cast your vote.

Share the Campaign

Spread the word, use hashtags and share them on social media to encourage others to participate.

Use TK Voting Guide

Our TK VT fanbase has prepared a TK Voting Guide for few platforms. Please utilize it!

Join the Team

Work together with organized fanbases to maximize every voting event. Reach out to Taekook Universe to join the voting group on X.

Vote Fairly

Avoid VPNs, bots, or macros that may invalidate votes. Always do it the right way!

  • Vote only through official platforms.
  • Read each platform's rules carefully.
  • Avoid VPNs and automated voting tools.
  • Complete email verification when required.
  • Join organized voting teams for reminders.
  • Use TK Voting Guide for detailed tutorials.
  • Every vote counts—don't miss daily resets.
