📖 GUIDE 06

Reporting

Learn how to report malicious or inappropriate content, impersonation, piracy, and other activities that disparges Taehyung and Jung Kook's names and work.

{% include "components/banner.html" %}
CHAPTER 01

Why Reporting Matters

Inappropriate and unethical use of Taehyung and Jung Kook's identity can damage their reputation and negatively affect the fandom. Reporting these activities helps protect both artists and creates a safer online community for everyone.

CHAPTER 02

What Should Be Reported?

Inappropriate Content

Posts, videos, images, or comments that disrespect or degrade their dignity.

Fake Accounts

Accounts especially newly created, impersonating Taehyung, Jung Kook, BTS members, or people closely associated with them.

Unauthorized Merchandise

Illegal products or merchandise sold without official authorization.

Defamatory Content

False accusations, hate speech, cyberbullying, malicious edits, or spreading rumors.

Piracy

Illegal distribution or unauthorized sharing of music, videos, or paid content.

CHAPTER 03

Where to Report?

Weverse

Report inappropriate posts or comments directly through the "Report" option.

BigHit Music

Email evidence and details to the official protection team.

X

Use X's Safety Center to report abusive accounts or harmful posts.

YouTube

Report videos, comments, or channels violating YouTube's policies.

Other Platforms

Use the built-in Report option available on the platform you're using.

CHAPTER 04

Reporting Best Practices

Be Responsible

Confirm the content is genuinely harmful before submitting a report.

Don't Engage

Avoid interacting with clout chasers or fan wars. Report, mute, and block instead.

Save Evidence

Always collect screenshots, links, usernames, and timestamps before reporting.

Be Timely

Coordinate with fanbases so reports are submitted quickly and effectively.

Raise Awareness

Share with your fellow fans to report harmful content collectively for a stronger impact.

CHAPTER 05

Need Help?

If you come across harmful content targeting Taehyung or Jung Kook and need assistance, reach out to Report for JK & Tae. The team can help verify reports, provide guidance, and coordinate community reporting efforts.

  • Verify information before reporting.
  • Take screenshots before content is removed.
  • Do not engage with harmful accounts.
  • Report, mute, and block.
  • Coordinate with Report for JK & Tae.
