CHAPTER 01

Why Radio Matters?

Radio airplay introduces Taehyung and Jung Kook's music to casual listeners who may discover their songs for the first time. It also increases visibility, supports chart performance, and expands their global reach.

🌎

Reach New Listeners

Radio introduces their music beyond existing fandom spaces and reaches casual audiences.

📻

Chart Impact

Radio exposure contributes to important music chart achievements.

🎵

Music Discovery

Many listeners discover new artists and songs through radio.

💜

Fan Support

Every request shows stations that fans want to hear their music.

CHAPTER 02

How to Request Songs

Requesting their songs is simple. Follow station guidelines and make your requests count.

📝

Request Forms

Visit station websites, find the song request section, and submit the artist and song title.

📱

Social Media Requests

Request songs through station posts on X, Instagram, Facebook, and other platforms.

🎧

Radio Apps

Apps like iHeartRadio, TuneIn, and other platforms may offer request features.

☎️

Live Requests

Some DJs accept listener requests during live shows. Always be polite and respectful.

💜

Fanbase Campaigns

Our TK Radio fanbase organizes radio request campaigns. Join their projects!

📍

Local Stations

Explore local radio stations and check their websites or social media for request options.

CHAPTER 03

Effective Requesting Tips

Follow these practices to support radio play effectively.

✨

Be Polite

Always communicate professionally with radio stations and DJs.

🎵

Be Specific

Mention the exact song title and artist name in every request.

🚫

Avoid Spam

Do not send repeated requests within a short period.

❤️

Engage Naturally

Like, share, and interact with station content.

🌎

Spread Awareness

Encourage other fans worldwide to participate.

📢

Join Projects

Participate in fanbase-led radio campaigns and challenges.
