CHAPTER 01

Why Streaming Matters?

Streaming Taehyung and Jung Kook's music on major platforms helps boost their chart rankings, increases visibility, and supports their music careers. Every stream counts, especially during new releases, a major comeback and important milestones.

CHAPTER 02

The Streamer's Handbook

🎧

Create an Account

Ensure you have a valid account on the platforms. If possible, use paid subscriptions for better impact.

🎵

Stream Their Music

Search for their official profiles (V / Jung Kook) and start from there.

📂

Create Playlists

Build personalized playlists with their songs and other artists to avoid repetitive patterns or use of playlists created by fanbases

🌱

Stream Naturally

Keep volume audible, interact normally, and stream like a human listener.

🔄

Avoid Looping

Create diverse playlists instead of repeating one song continuously.

🌎

Use Multiple Platforms

Support across different music platforms to maximize the impact.

📢

Share The Music

Encourage others to stream by sharing yours/Fanbases' playlists on social platforms.
